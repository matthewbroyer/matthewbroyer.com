Privacy

Last updated: September 2026

This page (matthewbroyer.com hub) is a free personal project. It does not require an account and does not use advertising or analytics scripts in this page’s code.

What this hub stores on your device

  • Theme preference (light/dark)
  • Pinned app IDs
  • Local launch counts for listed apps
  • Whether you dismissed the local-storage notice

These values are kept in your browser’s local storage for this site. This page does not transmit that local data to a remote application server.

What leaves your browser

  • Normal website hosting may log technical data such as IP address and user-agent (controlled by your host, not this HTML file).
  • Optional status checks request the listed app URLs from your browser to estimate reachability.
  • If you open a linked app or use the contact email link, your browser contacts those destinations.

Linked apps

Listed tools (for example mbmoney.online) are separate websites. Their storage and privacy practices are not fully controlled by this single hub page. Review each app you use.
